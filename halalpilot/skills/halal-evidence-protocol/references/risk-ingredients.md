# High-risk ingredient screening list

This is a screening aid, not a ruling. A HIGH or MEDIUM ingredient is not haram. It means
**the halal status depends on its source or process, so it needs a matching certificate**.

| Pattern (match name, code or description) | Risk | Why |
|---|---|---|
| gelatin / gelatine / E441 | HIGH | Often porcine or non-halal-slaughtered bovine |
| E471, E472a–f, E470–E483 (mono-/diglycerides, esters of fatty acids) | HIGH | Fat source may be animal |
| shortening, margarine, "vegetable fat" without a spec, lard, tallow | HIGH | Animal fat or blended carrier |
| flavour / flavouring / perisa / essence / extract | HIGH | Carriers may be ethanol or animal-derived; "seafood flavour" may contain non-halal components |
| vanilla extract, rum essence, wine, mirin, ang ciu, arak, cooking wine | HIGH | Alcohol (khamr) or alcohol-based carrier |
| enzymes, rennet, cheese, whey, whey powder | HIGH | Animal rennet or enzymes |
| L-cysteine / E920 | HIGH | Can be from hair or feathers |
| glycerol / glycerin / E422 | HIGH | Animal or plant fat |
| carmine / cochineal / E120 | HIGH | Insect-derived; acceptability depends on the scheme |
| meat, poultry, bone broth, stock, animal fat, chicken powder | HIGH | Needs slaughter and processing certification |
| collagen, bone char–refined sugar, natural casings | HIGH | Animal source |
| emulsifier / stabiliser / thickener without a code | HIGH | Unknown source |
| soy sauce, oyster sauce, vinegar, fermented sauces | MEDIUM | Fermentation by-products and additives |
| shrimp paste (belacan), fish sauce, dried seafood | MEDIUM | Processed; check additives and colouring |
| cooking oil, palm olein | MEDIUM | Processing aids and refining; a cert is normally available |
| sugar, salt, flour, spices (processed, packaged) | MEDIUM | Processing aids, anti-caking agents |
| fresh vegetables, fresh fruit, fresh chilli, shallots, garlic, plain water | LOW | Unprocessed plant material |
| dried chilli, tamarind (single-ingredient, no additives) | MEDIUM | Minimally processed; additives possible |

Unprocessed fish and seafood are generally regarded as halal, but **processed** or **flavoured**
seafood products still need a certificate.

If an ingredient matches nothing on this list, classify it `MEDIUM` and say so in `risk_reason`.
